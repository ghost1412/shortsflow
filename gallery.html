Clips made with ShortsFlow

Five captioned vertical clips (1080x1920) from one long, public-domain NASA video, made on a PC. The crop follows whoever is speaking and the captions are timed word by word.

Watch them

Each video is a 540x960 copy so it loads fast; the real clips are 1080x1920. Tap play on any of them.

Footage: NASA, public domain ("Podcast Live from Space: Astronaut Photography", Wikimedia Commons). It is an example of the output, not a claim about every video: results depend on the audio, the speakers and the picture.
